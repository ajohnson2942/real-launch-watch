How do I add this to my phone?

Watch-A-Launch

Apple

Open the Watch-A-Launch website on Safari. Click the three dots on the bottom right, and at the top click Share. Then click Add to Home Screen.

NTFY is a utility that you can add to your phone from your device's application downloading service. Once downloaded, make sure to follow the instructions in the homepage under Notification Location in order to get the notifications.

The exact wording of the install option can vary slightly by phone or browser version.
